# User action required

No dataset-dependent tutorial is complete. Provide only authorized, de-identified research inputs; never send secrets or patient records through chat.

- [ ] **P0 Mandatory for CNN:** Obtain labeled lesion images and usage permission from ISIC/HAM10000 (https://challenge.isic-archive.com/data/). Review the exact download license. Place images in `data/raw/lesions/images/` and CSV `data/raw/lesions/metadata.csv` with `image_path,label,group_id`. Supply the dictionary and whether groups represent patients or lesions. Blocks real training, architecture selection, held-out evaluation, Grad-CAM validation and prediction. ImageNet weights alone are insufficient.
- [ ] **P0 Mandatory for EHR:** Upload an authorized de-identified CSV/XLSX through dataset management, with source, license and dictionary specifying dates, units, ranges, encounter and patient keys. Obtain from your institution or individually credentialed MIMIC access (https://mimic.mit.edu/docs/faq/how-to-get-access.html). Blocks real EHR preprocessing and strategic indicators.
- [ ] **P0 Mandatory for forecasting:** Provide daily `date,count` CSV of observed arrivals, including explicit zero days, time zone and data dictionary. Obtain institution-approved aggregate operations data. Import as demand. Blocks real demand evaluation. For staffing also supply patients per staff per shift, shift duration and coverage policies; capacity scenarios are otherwise unavailable.
- [ ] **P0 Mandatory for NLP:** Provide de-identified `text` CSV, genuine `label` for classification and group key for repeated patient notes; import as clinical_text. MIMIC-IV-Note requires its own approval/DUA. Blocks real classification, clustering and retrieval evaluation. Supply a licensed clinical NER model with model card and local path to enable clinical entity extraction; no generic-model substitute.
- [ ] **P0 Mandatory for weight study:** Supply real adult numeric measurements, behaviors, outcome, confounders, units, missing codes and dictionary. CDC NHANES is a candidate (https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/). Import as weight. Specify survey cycle/design/weights for population inference; otherwise analysis is explicitly unweighted exploratory. Blocks T14–15 real findings.
- [ ] **P1 Mandatory for validated segmentation:** Supply corresponding expert mask PNGs and annotation/license provenance under `data/raw/masks/`. Without these, algorithms execute but clinical segmentation quality is unverified.
- [ ] **P1 Optional accelerator:** CPU is available; this process reports no CUDA/MPS. Provide GPU environment or approve sufficient CPU training time after dataset validation. Needed for practical model comparisons, not for deterministic image algorithms.
- [ ] **P1 Before shared deployment:** Supply host/TLS domain and identity provider/security requirements. Current token access is local single-owner research access; production RBAC, encryption at rest and institutional privacy review remain required before identifiable data.
- [ ] **P1 Mandatory for clinical claims:** Arrange clinician review, external validation and skin-tone/generalization assessment. No output may be used as a confirmed diagnosis.
- [ ] **P2 Optional terminology:** Confirm whether course 'HER' means EHR; current interpretation is provisional.
